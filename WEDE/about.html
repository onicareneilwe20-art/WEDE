About Checkers

Trusted since 1956 · Part of the Shoprite Group

Our History

Checkers opened its first supermarket in 1956 and has grown into a leading retailer across Southern Africa. We offer quality groceries, fresh produce, household essentials, and innovative services like Checkers Sixty60.

Mission: Provide quality products, exceptional value, and convenient shopping experiences through innovation.

Vision: To be the preferred supermarket retailer, delivering the best shopping experience.

Core Values

  • Customer satisfaction
  • Affordability & quality
  • Innovation & sustainability
  • Community development
  • Integrity & trust

Community: We support local initiatives and sustainable practices.

Customer commitment: We’re here to make your shopping easier, faster, and more rewarding.
